About Pagrin

The rewards and commerce layer for agent-native finance.

We're building the operating system institutions embed to turn every transaction — human or agent — into a relationship that lasts.

Our vision

Rewards were designed for a human tapping a card.

For two decades, rewards were designed for a human tapping a card. That assumption is breaking. Autonomous agents are beginning to shop, pay, and negotiate on people's behalf — and the institutions that hold the customer relationship risk becoming invisible plumbing.

Pagrin is a single, embeddable operating system for rewards and commerce. Institutions install it inside the apps and consoles they already run — no rebuild, no rip-and-replace — and get points, universal checkout, and always-on campaigns out of the box, governed from one place.

We built an operating system, not another point tool, because institutions don't need a tenth vendor — they need one governed layer that compounds. It is embedded by design, agent-native from the ground up, and secure for financial institutions, with every action scoped, consented, and logged.

Rebuilding the rewards and commerce layer for an agent-native world is a once-in-a-generation opening. Pagrin exists to win it — for the institutions, not around them.
