What it is

Native commerce, not a redirect.

Most “commerce” bolt-ons send your customer off to someone else's checkout — and hand over the relationship with it. Pagrin keeps the whole transaction inside your app: your brand, your data, your points, settled once. Everything below runs on the same rails and the same guardrails.

Agentic commerce protocols

Commerce between agents, not just people.

The next wave of transactions won't be a person clicking “buy” — it'll be their agent settling with yours. Pagrin supports machine-payment and agent-to-agent protocols, so agents can shop, run deep research and pay on a customer's behalf — always inside the rules you set.

Reachable like a person

Your customers text an agent. It answers like your best employee.

No menus, no ticket numbers, no waiting for opening hours. The agent has the account, the balance and the policy in front of it, and it can act — not just advise.
