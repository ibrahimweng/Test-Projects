Pagrin and Zenith Announce Strategic Partnership to Power Institutional Rewards and Engagement Across Canton Network

Pagrin, the rewards infrastructure layer for stablecoin neobanks and financial institutions, today announced a strategic partnership with Zenith, the canonical EVM/SVM layer of Canton, to bring next-generation loyalty and engagement solutions to institutional clients operating on Canton.

The partnership will enable Pagrin to service Canton's institutional clients through Zenith's infrastructure, while Zenith will act as a distribution partner for Pagrin's rewards platform across its growing network of Zenith Stack chain clients. This network currently has a pipeline of several hundred institutions preparing to issue, settle and service assets on Canton.

What the partnership delivers

Institutions building on Zenith Stack will be able to run a rewards programme without standing one up themselves. Pagrin reads balances and activity in place through the integrations an institution already has, decides what to offer per interaction rather than per batch, and settles the reward through the same rails the underlying transaction uses. Nothing has to be migrated for the programme to start.

  • Loyalty and reward balances that an agent or an application can spend directly, without a separate redemption journey.
  • Offers evaluated for each customer at the moment of the interaction, inside limits the institution sets once.
  • Scoped access, consent on spend and a complete audit trail on every action, so a risk team can read the programme end to end.
  • Distribution to Zenith Stack chain clients, so an institution can adopt the programme as part of the infrastructure it is already choosing.

Why Canton

Canton is being adopted by institutions that need privacy and control at the level of each transaction rather than a single shared ledger visible to everyone on it. Zenith provides the canonical EVM and SVM execution layer for that network, which lets teams build with familiar tooling while keeping the settlement guarantees Canton gives them.

For Pagrin that combination matters. A rewards programme touches customer data, spend authority and merchant settlement all at once, which is exactly the set of things an institution cannot expose broadly. Running on Zenith means the programme inherits those controls rather than working around them.
