The controls

Six controls, on by default.

These are not settings someone has to remember to switch on. They are the layer every agent action runs through, and they cannot be disabled per tenant.

Scoped access

Each agent holds a narrow, explicit permission set, per service and per customer. A request outside that scope is refused before it reaches your systems.

Consent on spend

Points and funds move only on the customer’s explicit approval. There is no configuration in which a balance changes silently.

Guardrails

Eligibility rules, spend limits, discount ceilings and brand policy are evaluated before an action runs, not inferred by the model afterwards.

Full audit trail

Every decision, offer and redemption is written to an append only log with its reasoning, and can be exported for inspection at any time.

Human in the loop

You choose which actions require a person. High value spend, unusual patterns and sensitive changes are routed with the full context to decide.

Data residency

Customer data stays in the region you nominate, encrypted with keys you control, with no copies taken outside it.

Our security posture

Pagrin sits inside the apps that regulated institutions already run, and it acts on money on behalf of their customers. That is a higher bar than most software has to clear, and it shaped the architecture rather than being added to it later.

The practical consequence is that the controls on this page are properties of the platform, not options. A tenant cannot turn off the audit log. An agent cannot ask for a permission it was not granted. There is one code path for acting on money, and it runs the same four checks every time.

We describe our posture from the architecture and the controls that are in place today. Where a certification is still in progress we say so plainly rather than implying otherwise.

Data handling

Your organisation keeps ownership of its data. We read it in place through the integrations you configure. We do not take a second copy into a separate warehouse, which is what removes both the pipeline lag and a whole class of governance problems that come with copies.

What we hold

  • Configuration you create, such as guardrails, offers, tiers and agent scopes.
  • The audit log of what each agent did, which includes references to customers and amounts.
  • Operational telemetry such as latency, error rates and request volumes.

What we do not hold

  • Full card numbers. Card data is tokenised by our payment partners and never reaches our systems.
  • Credentials for your core systems beyond the scoped tokens you issue us.
  • Copies of your customer records outside the region you nominate.

Retention and deletion

Retention windows are set per institution and enforced automatically. Deletion requests propagate through the platform. The audit log is the one exception, because records there are retained for the period your regulator requires, and we will document that period in your agreement.

Data residency and encryption

You nominate the region your data is held in and it stays there. Processing happens in the same region, so data does not cross a boundary in order to be acted on.

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or above, using modern cipher suites only. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. Keys are held in a managed key service in your region, and we support customer managed keys where your policy requires them.

Secrets used by the platform itself are held in a secrets manager, rotated on a schedule, and never written to logs or source control.

Infrastructure and resilience

The platform runs on hardened cloud infrastructure with strict separation between production, staging and development. There is no path from a development environment to production data.

Production is deployed across multiple availability zones. Traffic is load balanced, and services are designed to fail closed. If a check cannot be evaluated, the action does not run.

Changes to production go through code review, automated testing and staged rollout. Every deployment is attributable to a person and a change record, and can be rolled back.

Access and identity

Internal access follows least privilege. Access to production is time bound, approved by a second person, logged, and requires multi factor authentication. Nobody has standing access to customer data.

Administrative actions taken inside your console are recorded in the same audit trail as agent actions, so a change to a guardrail is as visible as a redemption.

On your side we support single sign on through your identity provider, with SCIM provisioning and role based permissions, so that the people who can change a guardrail are exactly the people you decided should be able to.

Application security

Security is part of the development process rather than a gate at the end of it.

  • Every change is reviewed by someone other than its author before it merges.
  • Dependencies are scanned continuously, and known vulnerable versions block a build.
  • Static analysis and secret scanning run on every commit.
  • We threat model any feature that touches money, consent or customer data before it is built.
  • An independent penetration test is run annually and after any significant architectural change, with a retest after remediation.

AI and model governance

The platform uses machine learning models, so the way those models are governed is a security question and not only a product one.

  • Customer data is never used to train models that are shared with other customers. Where we fine tune, we do it on your data, for you, inside your boundary.
  • Model outputs are treated as proposals. Nothing an agent decides becomes an action until the four checks have passed.
  • Prompts, retrieved context and outputs are logged with the action they produced, so a decision can be reconstructed after the fact.
  • We evaluate models against a fixed set of safety and accuracy tests before a version is promoted, and we keep the results.
  • Prompt injection is treated as an untrusted input problem. Content retrieved from a merchant, a document or a customer message cannot widen an agent’s scope.

Agent controls in the product

The six controls described at the top of this page are enforced by the platform on every action. The four checks that run before anything happens are scope, consent, guardrail and audit, in that order. If any of the first three fails, the action is refused or held for a person, and the attempt is still logged.

You can see this working, with a live audit log and the held and blocked counts, on the Secure Layer page.

Incident response

We maintain a documented incident response plan with named roles, and we rehearse it. The plan covers detection, containment, eradication, recovery and the review afterwards.

If an incident affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay and within seventy two hours of confirming it, whichever is sooner. We will tell you what happened, what data was involved, what we have done, and what we recommend you do. A written post incident review follows once the immediate work is finished.

Alerting runs continuously and is monitored around the clock. Security events are triaged against a documented severity scale.

Business continuity and backups

Backups are taken continuously and stored encrypted in the same region as the primary data. Restore procedures are exercised on a schedule rather than assumed to work, and the results of each exercise are recorded.

Our recovery point objective is fifteen minutes and our recovery time objective is four hours. Where your agreement sets tighter figures, those apply instead.

Personnel and training

Everyone at Pagrin goes through background screening appropriate to their role before they start, and signs a confidentiality agreement.

Security training happens at induction and annually after that, with additional training for engineers on secure development and on the specific risks of agent systems. Access is removed on the day someone leaves.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue, please tell us before you tell anyone else. Write to Hello@pagrin.com with the steps to reproduce it and any supporting detail.

Here is what we commit to.

  • We acknowledge your report within one working day.
  • We give you an assessment and a target fix date within five working days.
  • We keep you updated while we work on it, and tell you when it is fixed.
  • We will credit you publicly if you would like us to.
  • We will not pursue researchers who act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it.

Please do not test against production tenants, access data that is not yours, or run denial of service tests. If you need an environment to test in, ask us and we will set one up.
