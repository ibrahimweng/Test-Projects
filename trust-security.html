The controls

Six controls, on by default.

The same six that make up the Secure Layer. They are described here in the terms a risk or security team will want to assess.

Scoped access

Each agent holds a narrow, explicit permission set — per service and per customer. A request outside that scope is refused before it reaches your systems.

Consent on spend

Points and funds move only on the customer's explicit approval. There is no configuration in which a balance changes silently.

Guardrails

Eligibility rules, spend limits, discount ceilings and brand policy are evaluated before an action runs, not inferred by the model afterwards.

Full audit trail

Every decision, offer and redemption is written to an append-only log with its reasoning, and can be exported for inspection at any time.

Human-in-the-loop

You choose which actions require a person: high-value spend, unusual patterns, sensitive changes. Those are routed with full context.

Data residency

Customer data stays in the region you nominate, encrypted with keys you control, with no copies taken outside it.

Compliance

Pagrin is architected around the frameworks institutional buyers already assess against: SOC 2, PCI-DSS and regional data-residency requirements. These are designed into the platform rather than layered on top of it, which is why the controls above cannot be disabled per tenant.

Security posture is described here from the platform's architecture and controls. Certification status is shared under NDA during evaluation — ask for the security pack and we will send the current position along with the evidence behind it.

Data handling

Institutions keep ownership of their customer data. Pagrin reads it in place through the integrations you configure; it does not take a second copy into a separate warehouse, which is what removes both the pipeline lag and a whole class of governance problems.

  • Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or above, and at rest with keys held in the region you nominate.
  • Retention windows are set per institution and enforced automatically.
  • Customer data is never used to train shared models. Fine-tuning happens on your data, for you, inside your boundary.
  • Deletion requests propagate through the platform, the audit log excepted, where records are retained for the period your regulator requires.

Infrastructure

The platform runs on hardened cloud infrastructure with environment separation between production, staging and development. Production changes go through review, automated testing and staged rollout, and every deployment is attributable to a person and a change record.

Backups are taken continuously and restore procedures are exercised on a schedule, not assumed to work.

Access & identity

Internal access follows least privilege. Access to production is time-bound, approved, logged, and requires multi-factor authentication. Administrative actions inside your console are recorded in the same audit trail your agent actions are.

On your side, Pagrin supports SSO through your identity provider and role-based permissions so that the people who can change a guardrail are the people you decided should be able to.

Subprocessors

We maintain a current list of subprocessors, what each one processes and where. The list is provided with the security pack, and material changes are notified in advance so you have time to object.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue, please tell us before you tell anyone else. Write to Hello@pagrin.com with the steps to reproduce it. We acknowledge reports within one working day, keep you updated while we investigate, and will not pursue researchers who act in good faith and give us reasonable time to fix the issue.
