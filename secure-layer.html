The model

Six controls, on by default.

Not toggles you remember to switch on — the enforced layer every agent answers to.

Consent on spend

Points and funds move only when the customer approves. No silent deductions, ever.

Full audit trail

Every action, decision and redemption is logged, timestamped and exportable.

Scoped access

Every agent operates inside the permissions you set — per service, per customer. Nothing beyond what you allow.

Data residency

Your keys, your region, your controls — data stays where you require it.

Human-in-the-loop

Approval gates and escalation paths for anything high-stakes.

Compliance-ready

Architected for SOC 2, PCI-DSS and regional data-residency requirements — built into the platform, not bolted on.
